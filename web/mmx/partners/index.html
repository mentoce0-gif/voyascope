One trip to Phobos, many countries

Japan's MMX spacecraft left Earth on 20 October 2026. It is heading for Phobos, a moon of Mars, to bring a piece of it back home. Parts of it — and the people who will fly it — come from around the world.

VOYASCOPE is an unofficial, fan-made site. It is not connected with JAXA, NASA, CNES, DLR, ESA or any other agency. Every fact below links to the official page it comes from — please check there for the latest.
