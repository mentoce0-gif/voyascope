|---|---|---|---|
| 日時 2026-11-18 2:16:07 PST ＝ `2026-11-18T10:16:07Z`（日本時間 19:16:07） | [NASA「Where Are Voyager 1 and Voyager 2 Now?」](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/voyager/where-are-voyager-1-and-voyager-2-now/) | "On Wednesday, Nov. 18, 2026, at 2:16:07 a.m. PST, NASA's Voyager 1 spacecraft will be 16.094 billion miles (25.902 billion kilometers) away from Earth — or, the distance light travels in 24 hours, also known as one light-day." | ✅（PST＝UTC−8） |
